Privacy Policy

Free Rider Community's Account Centre

Effective date: Oct 10, 2026

Introduction

Account Centre is a free, open-source desktop application developed by the Free Rider Community to provide additional account management functionality for Free Rider HD.

We respect your privacy and do not collect, track, or remotely store your personal information.

Data Collection and Sharing

We do not collect, sell, share, or store your personal data on our servers. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Local Data Storage

We store only the information necessary for the application's functionality on your device, including:

  • Non-sensitive account information
  • Application settings and preferences
  • An encrypted authentication session token

All locally stored data remains on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

Data Management

You can access your account information through the application and remove locally stored data by signing out or clearing the application's data.

Network Communication

We communicate directly with Free Rider HD only to authenticate your session, retrieve account information, or perform requested account operations.

Information transmitted to Free Rider HD is subject to Kano Applications' Privacy Policy. We do not route this information through our own servers or transmit it for unrelated purposes.

Disclaimer

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Kano or Free Rider HD. Account Centre is an independent, unofficial application.
