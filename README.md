# Account Centre

A Windows utility for **Free Rider HD** that provides additional account and data management tools that aren't natively available in-game.

## Features
- Change your email
  <sub>Update the email address associated with your account.</sub>
- Remove your tracks
  <sub>Delete tracks from your account that you no longer want.</sub>
- Delete your races
  <sub>Remove races from your account and clean up your race history.</sub>
- Manage your account data
  <sub>Access account-management functionality from a simple Windows desktop application.</sub>

## Requirements
- Windows 10/11 x64

## Disclaimer

This application is an **unofficial third-party tool** and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by **Kano**.

Use the tools carefully. Actions such as deleting tracks, races, or account data may be permanent and cannot be undone.
